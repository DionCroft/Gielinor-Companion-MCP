# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
constructive, patient, and mindful that players and contributors have different
backgrounds and accessibility needs.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's
private information.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's
reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical.
